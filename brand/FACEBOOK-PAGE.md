## About / Description

**EN**

> Landlord is a free property management workspace for land and apartment owners in Bangladesh. Draw your plot boundaries on a map, keep apartments and tenant details together, record rent month by month, and keep deeds and other property PDFs next to the property they belong to. You can choose to encrypt documents on your device before upload. Landlord works in English and Bangla.
>
> Landlord is a record-keeping tool. It is not a marketplace, it does not collect rent payments, and it does not replace a land survey or legal advice.

## Content rules (from BRAND-GUIDE.md)

- Don't invent user counts, testimonials, savings or security certifications.
- Label rent figures as illustrative. The artwork already says "illustrative example" or "উদাহরণ মাত্র".
- Say "recorded rent", not "collected payment". The app records rent; it doesn't process payments.
- For encryption, say it is optional and that the passphrase must be kept safe. Never write "100% secure".

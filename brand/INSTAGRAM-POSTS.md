# Instagram launch posts

The 10 launch posts from [FACEBOOK-POSTS.md](FACEBOOK-POSTS.md), adapted for Instagram. The artwork is the same except for 03 and 04: Instagram has no text-only posts, so those two get image cards in `assets/instagram/posts/`.

Profile name, bio and Bangla bio are in [SOCIAL-CONTENT.md](SOCIAL-CONTENT.md). Use `assets/logos/social-avatar.png` as the profile picture.

**Links:** links in Instagram captions aren't clickable. Each caption ends with "link in bio", and the bio link is:

`https://www.landlordsbd.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=property_in_order&utm_content=bio`

Stories can carry a link sticker with their own `utm_content`.

**Videos:** every video has a synthetic voiceover over original music (credits in BRAND-GUIDE.md). Instagram publishes every video as a reel. For each one, upload the subtitles in the editor (or turn on auto-captions and check the Bangla), and pick the cover frame named below. The profile grid crops covers to 3:4. The scene headlines sit inside that crop, but the logo at the very top doesn't.

## 08 · Story (3 frames)

Post the three frames as three stories.

1. **Frame 1:** add a **Poll** sticker above "উত্তর দিন ↑". Options: "কাগজের ফাইলে" / "ফোনে বা কম্পিউটারে".
2. **Frame 2:** no sticker.
3. **Frame 3:** add a **Link** sticker in the open middle area, linking to `https://www.landlordsbd.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=property_in_order&utm_content=ig_08_story`. Sticker text: "ফ্রিতে শুরু করুন".

Instagram stories cover the top ~250 px with the profile bar and the bottom ~250 px with the reply field. The small logo at the top of each frame sits under the profile bar, which is fine because the profile name shows there. The headlines and sticker areas sit between the two bars. Save the stories to a highlight called **শুরু করুন / Start**, using `social-avatar.png` as the highlight cover.

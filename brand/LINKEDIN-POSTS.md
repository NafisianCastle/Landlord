**About:**

> Landlord is a free property management workspace for land and apartment owners in Bangladesh, including families who share property responsibilities and owners who manage property from another city or from abroad.
>
> Draw plot boundaries on a map, keep apartments and tenant details together, record rent month by month, and keep deeds and other property PDFs next to the property they belong to. Documents can optionally be encrypted on your device before upload. Landlord works in English and Bangla.
>
> Landlord is a record-keeping tool. It is not a marketplace, it does not collect rent payments, and it does not replace a land survey or legal advice.

## 04 · Text post: five habits

> Five simple habits for keeping property records in order:
>
> 1. Keep a list of every plot and flat, with its address and ownership details.
> 2. Keep scanned copies of deeds, khatian records and receipts in one place.
> 3. Write down rent as soon as you receive it, every month.
> 4. Keep each tenant's name, phone number and agreement date close at hand.
> 5. Once a year, check that your records and papers still match.
>
> None of these needs special software. A notebook works. Landlord just makes it easier to keep them all in one place. Link in the comments.
>
> #PropertyManagement #Bangladesh #Productivity
